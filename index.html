HOW IT WORKS

A complete system, not just a reminder

Six steps from setup to your first Gmail draft — all inside tools you already use.

1
Paste the script once
Open your Google Sheet → Extensions → Apps Script → paste the FollowUpFlow script → save. A new "🔁 FollowUpFlow" menu appears in your sheet. You never touch the code again.
One-time setup · no coding required
2
Add your proposals
Fill in your client's name, email, the service you proposed, and — most importantly — the Context field. This is where you write what you discussed, the client's situation, any specific concerns. The AI uses this to write something that sounds human and relevant.
Status and Follow-Up Count auto-fill when you type an ID
3
Set your follow-up date (or let automation do it)
Enter your own date in the Next Follow-Up column if you know when to reach out — it will never be overwritten. Or leave it blank and FollowUpFlow calculates a date automatically: Proposal Date + 3 days for the first follow-up.
Your date always wins · automation fills blank cells only
4
Generate follow-up drafts
Click FollowUpFlow → Generate Follow-Up Drafts. It scans all proposals that are due, calls Gemini AI to write a personalised email using your Context, and creates a Gmail draft — addressed, subject line included, body written. Check the Automation Notes column to see exactly what happened for every row.
AI output saved before Gmail is touched — safe if anything fails
5
Review and send from Gmail
Open Gmail → Drafts. Your email is there. Read it. Edit it if you want. Send it. FollowUpFlow never sends anything automatically — every email that goes out is one you've personally reviewed.
6
Mark it sent — everything updates automatically
After sending, set Follow-Up Sent to Yes in your sheet and run Process Sent Follow-Ups. The count increments, the sent date is recorded, and the next follow-up is scheduled — 3 days later for the second, 5 days for the third. After three follow-ups, the cycle ends automatically.
Maximum 3 follow-ups · lifecycle stops automatically
